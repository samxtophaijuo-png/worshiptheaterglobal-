Who We Are

Worship Theater Global Ministries

Worship Theater Global Ministries is a Christ-centered ministry committed to worship, the Word, spiritual transformation, discipleship and Kingdom mission.


We believe that genuine worship should lead people to the Word, the Word should produce spiritual power, power should produce discipleship, and discipleship should produce mission.

Our Vision & Mission

Romans 12:1–2

Our Vision

To see lives transformed through worship, the Word, discipleship and the demonstration of God's power.

Our Mission

To raise Christ-centered disciples who influence their generation and advance God's Kingdom in every sphere of life.

Our Calling

Repositioning the mindset of young people through the transforming power of God's Word.
